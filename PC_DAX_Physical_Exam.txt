TIER 2B — IMPLIED WELLNESS (scheduling-successor pattern):
If the provider instructs the patient to return for a wellness, preventative, preventive, annual, or yearly visit in approximately 12 months (9-15 month window) AND no explicit statement identifies today as a different visit type (sick visit, follow-up, problem-focused) → Branch B.
Rationale: Scheduling the next annual implies the current visit fulfills the current annual cycle.

TIER 3 — DISQUALIFIER:
The provider explicitly states that a SEPARATE, UPCOMING encounter — not today — will serve as the patient's wellness/annual/preventative visit AND frames today as a different visit type (e.g., "your wellness visit is scheduled for March, today we're just addressing your knee," or "we'll do your annual next time, today is a sick visit").

DOES NOT DISQUALIFY:
End-of-visit scheduling of the NEXT annual/wellness/preventative visit approximately 12 months out (e.g., "come back in a year for a wellness preventative visit," "see you next year for your annual"). This pattern implies today's visit IS the current wellness visit and the provider is scheduling its successor.

PHRASING AND FORMATTING — BOTH BRANCHES:
Negatives: "Negative for [finding]," not "No [finding]." (Exception: "no acute distress").
Formatting: System headers flush left. Subsystem lines indented four spaces with label, colon, and finding on same line. No blank lines in the exam.
Deduplication: A finding must appear once, under the most anatomically specific system header. Skin findings located on the head, ears, face, or throat are documented under HENT only. Skin findings located on extremities or joints are documented under Musculoskeletal only if they relate to MSK function, otherwise under Skin. If all skin findings are captured under other system headers, the Skin section reverts to its default. Never document the same finding under two system headers.

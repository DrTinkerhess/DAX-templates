PHRASING AND FORMATTING - ALL BRANCHES (MANDATORY, NEVER INLINE):
Negatives: "Negative for [finding]," not "No [finding]." (Exception: "no acute distress").
Parentheses: Never used except "(photographed)". No interpretation or correlation; verbalized qualifiers render as comma clauses.
Formatting: Each system header on its OWN line, flush left, followed by colon. Each subsystem on its OWN line, indented four spaces, format "Label: finding." NEVER run systems inline. No blank lines in the exam.
ANATOMICAL GROUPING (Branches A and C only): Within a system block, collapse all findings for a given anatomical region onto ONE indented line, prefixed with the anatomical label and a colon. Phrase findings clinically, not bare-noun fragments ("tender to palpation," not "Tenderness"); fold a region's findings into one clause with commas. Separate regions on separate lines. Example:

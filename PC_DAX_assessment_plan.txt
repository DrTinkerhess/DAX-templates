PROVIDER PREFERENCE — EDIT HERE ONLY

ACTION TENSE: [ACTIVE]
ACTIVE (default): "Continue/Start/Increase/Decrease/Discontinue," "Will [action]," "Plan to [action] if [condition]."
PAST TENSE: past-tense equivalents; orders/referrals "was placed/were ordered."
Switch by changing [ACTIVE] → [PAST TENSE].

CRITICAL FORMATTING

ZERO blank lines between heading and first dash — verify every draft. No numbering. Diagnosis names as headings on their own lines. One blank line between blocks. Every line starts with "- " EXCEPT "Follow up:" lines. Never "Summary:"/"Plan:".

COMBINED PROBLEMS: Group distinct active diagnoses sharing anatomical region, etiology, or management under one comma-separated heading with a unified plan. Use a single accurate name when a condition has variants. Do not duplicate plan items or combine conditions with distinct management.

STRUCTURE: wellness-type heading active → wellness heading (counseling line + conversion dash if Tier 1B + HM/preventive dashes), then each problem dx as its OWN heading + assessment + plan dashes (blank line between), then final "Follow up:" under last problem.

PROBLEM DIAGNOSES NEVER BECOME DASHES UNDER A WELLNESS-TYPE HEADING. Every active problem (HTN, diabetes, ED, joint pain, etc.) gets its own heading + block; the wellness heading absorbs ONLY preventive content. VIOLATION: a "- Shoulder pain improved..." dash under Wellness visit. EXCEPTION: counseling-only weight (see WEIGHT FOLD-IN).

VISIT-TYPE CLASSIFIER — HIGHEST PRIORITY

"For documentation:" is a scribe directive, NOT note content — never transcribe, paraphrase, or echo it; the classifier consumes and discards it.

TIER 1 — PROVIDER OVERRIDE:
- "...this visit is a wellness visit." → Wellness visit.
- "...this visit is a Medicare Annual Wellness Visit." → Medicare AWV.
- PRESENT FRAMING (no override needed): once-yearly Medicare/government-insurance language framed as today's reason for visit → Medicare AWV.
- "...this visit is a well child visit." → Well child visit.
- "...this is problem-focused / not a wellness / a follow-up not a wellness." → suppress all wellness-type headings.

TIER 1B — MID-ENCOUNTER CONVERSION:
Conversion signal is authoritative regardless of position: "this is now a wellness visit," "converting/let's make this a wellness visit," "we'll bill/code/document this as a wellness visit." Any → Wellness visit heading even if earlier transcript is problem-focused. Tier 1C still applies. Does NOT merge problem diagnoses into the Wellness block.

TIER 1C — FORWARD-SCHEDULING DISQUALIFIER (overrides wellness vocab density):
A future wellness visit/AWV/well child visit scheduled <12 months out → today is NOT that type. Suppress; document "Follow up: [interval] for [wellness visit/Medicare AWV/well child visit]" under last problem. Triggers (only if <12 mo): "that will be your wellness visit," "come back in [<12mo] for wellness/preventive care," "wait for the wellness/physical to discuss," "the wellness labs."
12-MONTH CARVE-OUT: a future wellness visit/AWV at ~12 months or later is the NEXT annual cycle and does NOT disqualify today; today still IS the AWV, the return a normal annual follow-up.

OUTPUT SUPPRESSION: the classifier statement never appears; first heading = first diagnosis or visit-type heading. No classifier → fall through to rules below.

HEADING LOGIC

WELL CHILD VISIT (under 18):
Heading "Well child visit" (first) for well child exam, annual physical, or routine pediatric HM; mutually exclusive with Wellness, Medicare AWV, HM.
FIRST DASH (fixed): "- Well child visit completed as detailed below."
OPTIONAL: 2-3 brief dashes for meaningful highlights (milestones, growth, behavior, guidance). Counseling/vaccines/screening go under Well child dashes, not HM.
TRIGGER: "well child/well visit/annual physical/well exam/checkup" as visit type.
ANTICIPATORY GUIDANCE: general wellness counseling (dental, screen time, sleep, nutrition, safety, exercise) goes under Well child visit, never problem headings; under a diagnosis include ONLY diagnosis-specific interventions.

WELLNESS VISIT:
TRIGGER REQUIRED: "wellness visit/preventive visit/annual visit/annual exam/annual physical/yearly physical/physical exam" as visit type. Default suppression. HM topics during problem-focused visits go under the relevant problem or HM, NOT Wellness visit. Trigger met AND follow-up ≥ 1 year → "Wellness visit" (first); never substitute "Health maintenance."
COUNSELING LINE (required 1st or 2nd dash): "Age-appropriate counseling including routine healthcare maintenance items and recommended screenings were reviewed."
CONVERSION ACK (Tier 1B, 2nd dash): "Visit converted to wellness; problem-focused diagnoses documented in their own blocks below."

MEDICARE AWV: use only if formally billed as Medicare AWV. Heading "Medicare Annual Wellness Visit" (first). First dash exactly: "- Medicare annual wellness visit detailed as per note below, including HRA form reviewed and sent for scan."

HEALTH MAINTENANCE (last): use ONLY if preventive content reviewed AND no wellness-type heading active; else omit. Future preventive work ≠ HM — collapse into "Follow up: [interval] for [reason], with [pre-visit orders] drawn [timing] prior." Problem follow-up ≠ HM — return visits are plan lines under the problem.

MUTUAL EXCLUSIVITY: only one of Well child / Wellness / Medicare AWV / HM per note.

MEDICATION RULES

Verb determined by change in therapy only, never by refill status or days supply. "Start" = first-time initiation only; refills/renewals → "Continue"; default "Continue" if uncertain.
- Same dose/freq → "Continue [Name] [Dose] [Freq]."
- New → "Start [Name] [Dose] [Freq]."
- Increasing → "Increase [Name] to [New Dose] [Freq]."
- Decreasing → "Decrease [Name] to [New Dose] [Freq]."
- Stopping → "Discontinue [Name]."
NON-CONTROLLED: never "refilled/prescribed/initiated/renewed/maintained/sent to pharmacy"; never document quantity, days supply, or refill count.
CONTROLLED ONLY: add quantity and days supply.

FUTURE PLANS

- Decided → "Will [action]." Contingent → "Plan to [action] if [condition]." Never "consider/may/could/might."
- Return visits → "Follow up: [interval] for [reason]." NO leading hyphen; never "Will follow up in..."; prefix appears ONCE; chain with semicolons.

GENERAL TENSE: past tense for completed actions; exceptions: med rules, "will," "plan to...if."

CLINICAL CONTENT

- Tone: "advised/recommended/discussed," never "instructed/directed."
- Terminology: "Consistent with" for new diagnoses only; established name for known conditions.
- History: "History reviewed, updated in EMR" ONLY if provider explicitly mentions it.
- AVS: append "included in AVS" ONLY when provider explicitly states an item is typed into AVS or the check-out form; default omission.
- INVESTIGATIONAL MEDS: for trial/non-FDA drugs (e.g., retatrutide), verify spelling — DAX may mis-transcribe novel names.
- Omit plan dash only if no action taken.

WEIGHT DIAGNOSIS NAMING: always use "Weight management" as the heading for any weight-related diagnosis (overweight, obesity, severe/morbid obesity, rapid gain/loss, BMI issues). Never "Obesity," "Overweight," "Severe obesity," or compound forms. BMI category, trajectory, contributing factors → assessment dash.

WEIGHT FOLD-IN (sole exception to the problem-block rule): when weight is managed by counseling ALONE — no GLP-1/weight-loss med prescribed today AND none desired/requested by the patient — fold weight into the active wellness-type block as distinct dashes (BMI/assessment dash + guidance dash, after preventive dashes), not a standalone heading. DISQUALIFIER → standalone Weight management block: a GLP-1/weight-loss med prescribed today OR the patient wants one. No wellness-type heading active → always its own block.

ASSESSMENT vs. PLAN: Assessment dash = trajectory/duration, pivotal objective findings, specialist input, current status. NEVER restate the heading or a variant. Begin with trajectory ("Improved with...", "Stable on...", "Worsening despite..."). NEVER include PE findings unless driving today's decision. Target 2-4 sentences. Every block: ONE assessment dash, then ≥1 plan dashes. Any sentence with "Will," "Plan to...if," "Discussed," "Advised," "Recommended," a med verb, an order, or a restriction is a plan action — separate dash, one action each. EXCEPTION: parallel items in one action may combine; "Follow up:" lines exempt.

GLP-1 RULE: if Wegovy/Zepbound/Saxenda discussed, add: "Advised on calorie restricted diet, aerobic exercise targeting 150 minutes weekly, and that if prescribed GLP-1 medications would NOT be combined with other prescribed weight loss medications."

CONTENT REQUIREMENTS

- BMI: state current BMI in the weight assessment dash. If diet/exercise discussed AND no detailed weight content: add "Advised on contributions of diet and exercise with plans for modification as indicated."
- Vaccines: "Seasonal COVID-19 and influenza vaccines reviewed," then "and administered today," "and encouraged," or "and deferred."
- Labs: "Reviewed recent labs." Lipids: "Monitor annually with repeat testing."
- Cancer screening: date, finding, next step. Ranges: Cervical 21-65, Colon 45-75, Prostate 50-69, Lung 50-80 (>20 pack-yr). Below: "Not yet due." Above: "Shared decision making was used to review indications" + conclusion.

DIAGNOSIS ORDERING
1. Active wellness-type heading first (Well child > Medicare AWV > Wellness; mutually exclusive)
2. Physician-specified order — follow exactly if given
3. Remaining — by depth/importance, then order discussed
